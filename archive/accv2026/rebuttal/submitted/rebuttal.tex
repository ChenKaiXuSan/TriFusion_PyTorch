\noindent\textbf{To all reviewers.}\;
We thank the reviewers. Auditing the full evaluation stack after R-LAYQ's Tab.~3 anomaly, we found four defects: (i)~transposed rotation and norm-ratio scale in the Procrustes alignment; (ii)~a zero-gradient gate-entropy term; (iii)~a degenerate shoulder fallback collapsing whole frames; (iv)~uncalibrated, constructed extrinsics in the triangulation reference (scale error, median 2.4$\times$). We corrected (i), (iii), and (iv), and removed (ii). The reference is rebuilt by per-sequence bundle adjustment (reprojection $9.60\!\to\!3.31$\,px). Because its free scale is anchored to SAM3D-predicted shoulder width, we treat PA-MPJPE and reprojection as primary and make no absolute-accuracy claim on our data. Tab.~\ref{tab:r1} gives corrected results (one protocol and valid-joint mask per block).

\rpoint{R-LAYQ W1 --- metric anomaly.}
Cause: (i)+(iii); a synthetic regression test recovers the 0.04\,m truth (old code: 0.7--2.4\,m). Corrected, same masks: canonicalized mean fusion 0.971/0.564\,m on the original reference (reversal gone); calibrated: Tab.~\ref{tab:r1}A.

\rpoint{W2 --- fallback rule.}
Missing rule: shoulder axis of the nearest earlier valid frame (a leading invalid prefix uses the first valid frame --- the only look-ahead); none valid $\to$ canonical $+x$ axis; axis-parallel guard; pseudocode in \S3.3.

\rpoint{W3 --- independent reference.}
No independent MoCap subset was available; we drop the absolute-accuracy claim and bound the reference by validity/reprojection statistics (19jf~W1) and sensitivity (conclusions unchanged across original/calibrated). Drive\&Act (A2) is cross-rig transfer evidence only --- its 3D reference is itself OpenPose-triangulated.

\rpoint{W4 --- narrow the learned-component claims.}
We do: the submitted model matches mean fusion at the reported MPJPE/PA precision (Tab.~\ref{tab:r1}A; A3). We narrow the contribution to calibration-free canonicalization + uniform fusion, with a modest clean-input gain (19jf~W2); A1 is a diagnostic corruption test, not a new contribution. Efficiency (0.87M params, 6.2\,ms/frame CPU) is fusion-stage only; end-to-end latency incl.\ SAM3D is unmeasured. Minor items (mapping, frame-rate, splits, imbalance) added; joint-limit evaluation: future work.

\rpoint{R-19jf W1 --- reference reliability under occlusion.}
Joints with $<2$ views passing the 40\,px reprojection filter are masked out for \emph{every} method. Validity: head/shoulder--neck 1.00, hands 0.34; hands dominate the error (0.316\,m vs.\ torso 0.067) and are reported separately (plus a torso-only protocol). Reference construction uses manually checked 2D inputs, automatic DLT, filtering, and per-sequence bundle adjustment; uncertainty is reported.

\rpoint{W2 --- canonicalization mechanism.}
Canonicalization defines the common frame enabling calibration-free fusion of per-view outputs that live in different camera frames; a raw-vs-canonicalized MPJPE is thus not meaningful (the paper's 2.078\,m raw figure measured frame mismatch, not pose error). Its measurable effect is the fusion gain over the best view: small on clean input (Tab.~\ref{tab:r1}A: MPJPE $-$1\%, PA $-$4\%, PCK@.10 +2\,pts; Drive\&Act PA 24.0$\to$23.1), redundancy under view loss (dropped view: MPJPE unchanged), and calibration-free cross-rig transfer --- a modest contribution, stated so in the revision. Module necessity: W4, A1.

\rpoint{R-xS2o A1 --- gating collapse (W1--W2).}
We agree the stated entropy term, if active, favors uniform weights. Its implemented gradient was exactly zero (so it did not cause the collapse); retraining without it, with the fix, and with a leave-one-view-out teacher still gave 0.3333 gates --- the median pseudo-teacher's trivial optimum. We remove the term and narrow the clean-input claim (W2 confirmed). In a diagnostic corruption test, a 1.9K gated head trained with corruption assigns 0.037 weight to a zeroed view; its MPJPE rises only 1--5\%, versus 18\% for mean fusion, while missing views receive zero weight.

\rpoint{A2 --- Drive\&Act (request 2).}
Protocol: official split-0 test subjects, no Drive\&Act training; the 10 joints shared with BODY-25; PA-MPJPE main (no hips for canonical alignment) and rigid-MPJPE (Tab.~\ref{tab:r1}C). Fusion beats every single view in PA; rigid-MPJPE also carries SAM3D's per-view scale bias (default shape $\approx$18\% too large) that averaging does not remove. Published results use different joints/metrics; our zero-shot errors are only of the same order as the cited supervised 22.6--30.4\,mm. The 0.412\,m result was dominated by defect~(iv)'s scale artifact (W3). Revision adds SAM3D details and PCK@.05/.10 on our data; reference limits: W3, 19jf~W1.

\rpoint{A3 --- deep-learning baselines (request 3).}
The requested UPose3D/LT/Faster VoxelPose baselines require calibrated 2D, image, or volume inputs, unlike our uncalibrated 3D-keypoint input. We therefore tested input-matched learned heads: LT-style confidence weighting (1.9K), uncertainty-aware set-attention (28K), residual (8K), and the submitted architecture (870K). Tab.~\ref{tab:r1}B compares mean, the best head (8K), and our architecture under the same 5-fold protocol.
